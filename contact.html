Our story

About Page & Paper

Page & Paper is a small, independent digital bookshop for people who love to read. We select a focused collection of e-books across fiction, mystery, science fiction, fantasy, history, business, technology and self-help.

Every title comes with a free sample chapter, so you can step inside a book before you decide to own it. Page & Paper is a fictional store created for a Web Programming college project.

Our mission is to make discovering a great book as calm and enjoyable as browsing a quiet shelf.

— The Page & Paper team
